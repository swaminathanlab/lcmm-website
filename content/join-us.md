# Join us

We are an interdisciplinary lab: engineers, physicists and biologists working side by side.
If you want to work on how physical forces and materials shape cell behaviour, get in touch.

## Postdocs with their own funding

We are happy to support fellowship applications (e.g. MSCA, EMBO, HFSP, VR, Wenner-Gren).
Send a CV, a short statement of what you want to work on and why here, and the names of
two references to [vinay.swaminathan@med.lu.se](mailto:vinay.swaminathan@med.lu.se).

## Master's and undergraduate students

We host degree projects from medicine, biology, engineering and physics programmes.
Email with your programme, the period you are available and what draws you to the lab.
